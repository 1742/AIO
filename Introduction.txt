[h1]All-in-One[/h1]
[b]Gestalt Crisis Ascension Mod[/b]

[hr]

[h1]Overview[/h1]
This mod provides a brand new Crisis Ascension path for [b]Gestalt Empires[/b] — [i][b]All-in-One[/b][/i]. Advance through Crisis Levels, apply DAIT operations, and complete [i][b]Yog-Sothoth[/b][/i] to ascend and master the laws of reality.

Although you will see many shadows of the vanilla ascension paths on this route [strike](What a cobbled-together crisis)[/strike], it now introduces a brand new [b]Directional Applied Infinity Thesis (DAIT)[/b] system. It is no longer just boring stat padding, but directly modifying the underlying code of the universe! [strike](Isn't that just a fancy way of saying buffs?...)[/strike]

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]New Ascension Perk: [b]All-in-One[/b] and a Become the Crisis path.
[*]New [b]DAIT[/b] (Directional Applied Infinity Thesis) buff system.
[*]New Origin: All-in-One.
[*]New Megastructure: Yog-Sothoth.
[/list]

[h2]Core Gameplay: Law Editing (DAIT)[/h2]
Just like the three vanilla Crisis paths, you need to adopt three Ascension Perks before you can select [b]All-in-One[/b]. By accumulating [b]Transcendental Logic[/b], you are no longer simply researching technologies, but directly converting logic into empire modifiers.
The current Crisis Buffs are divided into three categories. You can switch between them at any time.

[h2]Walkthrough[/h2]

[h3]Crisis Level 1[/h3]
Unlocked upon adoption:
[list]
[*][b]Exponential Learning Theory:[/b] Unlocks the capital's Matryoshka Brain District and uncapped Recursive Node jobs; Virtual empires can assemble purpose-built Recursive Chassis as material nodes.
[*][b]Horizon Engine:[/b] Produces Energy and Dark Matter.
[/list]

[h3]Crisis Level 2[/h3]
Unlocked after completing the [i][b]Horizon Engine[/b][/i]:
[list]
[*]Upgrade to the [i][b]Gate of All Gates[/b][/i]. Completing it creates [i][b]The Nameless Mist[/b][/i] and triggers one Random Applied Infinity Thesis.
[*]Unlock [b]Enrich[/b] and [b]Storm[/b] DAIT operations.
[/list]

[h3]Crisis Level 3-5[/h3]
Upgrade to [i][b]Silver Key[/b][/i] -> [i][b]Eternal Throne[/b][/i] -> [i][b]Yog-Sothoth[/b][/i].
Every upgrade triggers one Random Applied Infinity Thesis. Crisis Levels 3/4/5 require 1/3/6 cumulative DAIT uses and unlock the next operation pair. Completing Yog-Sothoth grants victory.
