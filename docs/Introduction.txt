[h1]All Is One[/h1]

[h2]Overview[/h2]
A new Become the Crisis path: [i][b]All Is One[/b][/i]. Use Directional Apply Infinity, build [i][b]Yog-Sothoth[/b][/i], and ascend to mastery over reality.

A [i]Cosmogenesis[/i] offshoot built around [b]Directional Apply Infinity Thesis[/b] (DAIT): give the universe a thorough overhaul.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]A new Ascension Perk, [b]All Is One[/b], and its Become the Crisis path
[*]A new series of [b]Directional Apply Infinity[/b] effects
[*]A new All Is One origin
[*]New Crisis megastructures
[/list]

[h2]Core Gameplay[/h2]
Adopt three Ascension Perks before choosing [b]All Is One[/b]. Spend [b]Hyperlogic[/b] to edit the universe and advance your Crisis progress. Cosmogenesis buildings and ships are included. [strike]What patch are we on, and we're still spamming repeatables and ships?[/strike]

[h2]Walkthrough[/h2]
Advance with Hyperlogic and megastructure milestones; see the Crisis panel for requirements. The route is: [b]Horizon Engine → Gate of Gates → Silver Key → Eternal Throne → Yog-Sothoth → Aforgomon Endgame → Choose Whether to Ascend[/b]. The special origin starts fully ascended and skips this progression.

[h3]Crisis Level 1: Start Burning Through Brains[/h3]
Start with these technologies:
[list]
[*][b]Exponential Learning Theory:[/b] Unlocks Matryoshka Brain districts and Recursive Nodes on all colonies; virtual empires can use physical Recursive Chassis. [strike]Isn't this just the Synaptic Lathe?[/strike]
[*][b]Dimensionality Hypothesis:[/b] Unlocks the [i][b]Horizon Engine[/b][/i] technology option. Its Energy keeps your synapses burning. [strike]So much for the Aetherophasic Engine.[/strike]
[*][b]Dark Matter Engineering:[/b] Unlocks Dark Matter technologies, production specializations and edicts for later megastructures.
[/list]
Technologies, ship-researched special projects and astral rifts also earn Hyperlogic. Don't just stare at the Brain.

[h3]Crisis Level 2: Open the Gate[/h3]
Complete the [i][b]Horizon Engine[/b][/i] and meet the Hyperlogic requirement to unlock its upgrade into the [i][b]Gate of Gates[/b][/i].
[list]
[*]The Gate creates the [i][b]Nameless Mist[/b][/i], where megastructure upgrades continue. It has no colony.
[*]Unlock your first [b]DAIT[/b] operations: [b]Enrich[/b] and [b]Storm[/b]. Enrich resolves instantly and adds deposits to systems; Storm makes the universe a little livelier.
[*]Unlock Living Metal production and edicts for later upgrades.
[/list]

[h3]Crisis Level 3[/h3]
Complete the [i][b]Gate of Gates[/b][/i] and meet the next level's requirements to unlock the [i][b]Silver Key[/b][/i].
[list]
[*]Completing the Silver Key unlocks [b]Generate[/b] and [b]Harvest[/b]: one rebuilds systems, while the other takes them apart for resources.
[*]Unlock Nanite production and related edicts.
[/list]

[h3]Crisis Level 4[/h3]
After completing the [i][b]Silver Key[/b][/i], continue toward the [i][b]Eternal Throne[/b][/i].
[list]
[*]Completing the Eternal Throne unlocks [b]Seed[/b] and [b]Strike[/b].
[*]Strike summons a weakened avatar of Aforgomon that scales with game difficulty and the Crisis Strength setting, [b]hostile to friend and foe alike[/b]. Don't leave your own fleets around to watch the show. Defeating an avatar you summoned this way does not complete the endgame.
[/list]

[h3]Crisis Level 5[/h3]
Complete the [i][b]Eternal Throne[/b][/i] and reach level five to unlock the final megastructure upgrade, [i][b]Yog-Sothoth[/b][/i]. The level-five reward [b]Transcend Causality[/b] reduces the post-cast cooldowns of Seed, Harvest, Strike and Erase.

Yog-Sothoth [b]automatically starts the Aforgomon endgame[/b], not ascension. Reality has noticed your edits, and its correction mechanism has come knocking.

[h3]How Do I Use the DAIT Menu?[/h3]
After the Gate of Gates, click the bottom orb to expand DAIT, or open it with the [b]Apply Infinity Menu[/b] edict.
[list]
[*]Select a target, choose an operation and applicable range, then click [b]Confirm[/b]. Entering system view alone does not select a target. In an erased, empty system, select the [b]Void Coordinate[/b] object and use Generate to rebuild it.
[*]The upper-right × collapses the menu and keeps the orb. [b]Exit Menu[/b] at the bottom right closes it completely; use the edict to reopen it.
[*]Before ascension, every operation costs Hyperlogic. Enrich resolves instantly; the others have cast times, with progress shown in their Situations.
[/list]

[h3]Endgame: Investigate, Induce, Constrain, Then Hit Back[/h3]
Situations show research progress, the advent target and remaining time.
[list]
[*][b]First, let it leave some remnants.[/b] Omens last 1800 days, shortening by 200 each round to a minimum of 200. An unconstrained avatar stays for 180 days, then erases the system and leaves remnants. Investigate each with a science ship and scientist for 360 days; each counts once.
[*][b]Investigate one remnant:[/b] Learn the advent cycle. [b]Investigate two:[/b] Unlock Induction. [b]Investigate three:[/b] Unlock Induction and Constraint together. The main research Situation will not complete itself while you idle.
[*][b]Bring it to a battlefield you've prepared.[/b] Select an external system, then click Induction or Constraint at the bottom of the DAIT menu. Induction costs 10,000 Hyperlogic. Constraint costs an additional 2,000 Dark Matter and also performs Induction. Both require 360 days of science-ship field research and must finish before manifestation. Redirecting the target does not reset the countdown.
[*][b]Don't let it come home.[/b] The first five rounds favor external systems; later rounds target the Nameless Mist, or sooner if no eligible external system remains. Redirect it: [b]arrival at the Nameless Mist causes immediate defeat[/b], erasing both the Mist and the Ultimate Gate.
[*][b]Now your fleets get their turn.[/b] A constrained avatar gives you a 720-day combat window and scales with game difficulty and the Crisis Strength setting. To win, you must defeat [b]the constrained avatar belonging to your endgame[/b] before its stay ends. Completing research, defeating an unconstrained avatar, or destroying an avatar summoned by Strike does not count. Run out of time, and the system is still erased.
[/list]

[h3]After Victory[/h3]
After defeating Aforgomon, choose [b]Step Beyond the Gate[/b] to proceed directly to the ascension ending, or [b]Pause and Contemplate[/b] to keep playing as your current empire.

Deferring grants a free, untimed [b]Ascension[/b] project. Research it for 30 days to revisit the choice; you can defer again.

[b]Your existing colonies are removed and your empire transformed only after you confirm ascension.[/b] Victory already unlocks Invert Ethics and Erase, even if you defer ascension; both still cost Hyperlogic, and Erase still has a cooldown. Confirming ascension upgrades Strike to its full avatar and makes every DAIT operation free and exempt from cooldowns. Operations with cast times still require you to wait. Empire-wide Strike records witnessing empires with fleets in the central system when casting begins, then spreads through their territory when it resolves. Nomadic witnesses also contribute eligible systems containing their fleets at that time. Each system is struck only once. Avatars remain hostile to all, so don't forget that just because you've ascended.
