<h2>Overview</h2>
This mod adds a new Become the Crisis path: <i><b>All Is One</b></i>. Advance through Crisis levels, use Directional Apply Infinity, and complete the dedicated megastructure <i><b>Yog-Sothoth</b></i> on your way to ascension and mastery over the laws of reality.
<br>
The ambition may sound a little like *Cosmogenesis*, but after the author's repeated tinkering, the mod now has its own <b>Directional Apply Infinity Thesis</b> system (DAIT), letting you give the universe a thorough overhaul.

<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>A new Ascension Perk, <b>All Is One</b>, and its Become the Crisis path</li>
<li>A new series of <b>Directional Apply Infinity</b> effects</li>
<li>A new All Is One origin</li>
<li>New Crisis megastructures</li>
</ul>

<h2>Core Gameplay</h2>
As with the three vanilla Crisis paths, you need to adopt three Ascension Perks before choosing <b>All Is One</b>. Spend <b>Hyperlogic</b> to edit the universe and advance your Crisis progress. As a Cosmogenesis offshoot, it comes with all the old-timer buildings and ships, though at their current power and cost efficiency, is anyone really using them...? (What patch are we on, and we're still spamming repeatables and ships?)

<h2>Walkthrough</h2>
Advancing Crisis levels requires both Hyperlogic and the preceding megastructure milestones; check the Crisis panel for the exact requirements. The route is: <b>Horizon Engine → Gate of Gates → Silver Key → Eternal Throne → Yog-Sothoth → Aforgomon Endgame → Choose Whether to Ascend</b>. The special origin starts fully ascended and skips this progression.

<h3>Crisis Level 1: Start Burning Through Brains</h3>
After adopting the perk, start with the dedicated technologies and your basic economy:
<ul>
<li><b>Exponential Learning Theory:</b> Unlocks the Matryoshka Brain district and Recursive Node jobs, without restricting them to the capital. Virtual empires can use dedicated physical Recursive Chassis. <del>Isn't this just the Synaptic Lathe?</del></li>
<li><b>Dimensionality Hypothesis:</b> Provides the technology option needed to build the <i><b>Horizon Engine</b></i>. The engine produces the Energy you need to keep burning through synapses. <del>So much for the Aetherophasic Engine.</del></li>
<li><b>Dark Matter Engineering:</b> Opens up Dark Matter technologies, production specializations and edicts, helping you prepare materials for later megastructures.</li>
</ul>
Researching technologies, completing ship-researched special projects and exploring astral rifts also earn Hyperlogic. Don't spend the whole game staring at the Brain.

<h3>Crisis Level 2: Open the Gate</h3>
Complete the <i><b>Horizon Engine</b></i> and meet the Hyperlogic requirement to unlock its upgrade into the <i><b>Gate of Gates</b></i>.
<ul>
<li>Completing the Gate of Gates creates the <i><b>Nameless Mist</b></i>, where you continue upgrading the dedicated megastructure. The Mist does not host a colony.</li>
<li>Unlock your first <b>DAIT</b> operations: <b>Enrich</b> and <b>Storm</b>. Enrich resolves instantly and adds deposits to systems; Storm makes the universe a little livelier.</li>
<li>This level also opens Living Metal production and related edicts. Start preparing materials for the next upgrades.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Crisis Level 3</h3>
Complete the <i><b>Gate of Gates</b></i> and meet the next level's requirements to unlock the <i><b>Silver Key</b></i>.
<ul>
<li>Completing the Silver Key unlocks <b>Generate</b> and <b>Harvest</b>: one rebuilds systems, while the other takes them apart for resources.</li>
<li>This level opens Nanite production and related edicts, keeping your megastructure upgrades supplied.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Crisis Level 4</h3>
After completing the <i><b>Silver Key</b></i>, continue toward the <i><b>Eternal Throne</b></i>.
<ul>
<li>Completing the Eternal Throne unlocks <b>Seed</b> and <b>Strike</b>.</li>
<li>Strike summons a weakened avatar of Aforgomon that scales with game difficulty and the Crisis Strength setting, <b>hostile to friend and foe alike</b>. Don't leave your own fleets around to watch the show. Defeating an avatar you summoned this way does not complete the endgame.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Crisis Level 5</h3>
Complete the <i><b>Eternal Throne</b></i> and reach level five to unlock the final megastructure upgrade, <i><b>Yog-Sothoth</b></i>. The level-five reward <b>Transcend Causality</b> reduces the post-cast cooldowns of Seed, Harvest, Strike and Erase.
<br>
Completing Yog-Sothoth <b>automatically starts the Aforgomon endgame</b>; it does not immediately ascend your empire. You've rewritten reality a little too freely, and the universe's correction mechanism has come knocking.

<h3>How Do I Use the DAIT Menu?</h3>
From the Gate of Gates onward, a fixed orb appears at the bottom of the screen. Click it to expand the menu, or use the <b>Apply Infinity Menu</b> edict to open it already expanded.
<ul>
<li>Select a target, choose an operation and applicable range, then click <b>Confirm</b>. Entering system view alone does not select a target. In an erased, empty system, select the <b>Void Coordinate</b> object and use Generate to rebuild it.</li>
<li>The upper-right × collapses the menu and keeps the orb. <b>Exit Menu</b> at the bottom right closes it completely; use the edict to reopen it.</li>
<li>Before ascension, every operation costs Hyperlogic. Enrich resolves instantly; the others have cast times, with progress shown in their Situations.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Endgame: Investigate, Induce, Constrain, Then Hit Back</h3>
The endgame displays both your research progress and the current advent Situation. Check the Situation for the target and remaining time.
<ul>
<li><b>First, let it leave some remnants.</b> The first warning period lasts 1800 days, shortening by 200 days each round to a minimum of 200. An unconstrained avatar stays for 180 days after arriving, then erases the system and leaves remnants to investigate. Send a science ship with a scientist for a 360-day field investigation. Each remnant counts only once.</li>
<li><b>Investigate one remnant:</b> Learn the advent cycle. <b>Investigate two:</b> Unlock Induction. <b>Investigate three:</b> Unlock Induction and Constraint together. The main research Situation will not complete itself while you idle.</li>
<li><b>Bring it to a battlefield you've prepared.</b> Select an external system, then click Induction or Constraint at the bottom of the DAIT menu. Induction costs 10,000 Hyperlogic. Constraint costs an additional 2,000 Dark Matter and also performs Induction. Both require 360 days of science-ship field research and must finish before manifestation. Redirecting the target does not reset the countdown.</li>
<li><b>Don't let it come home.</b> For the first five rounds, it looks for external systems; afterward, it targets the Nameless Mist. If no eligible external target can be found, it may turn back even earlier. Redirect it in time: <b>arrival at the Nameless Mist causes immediate defeat</b>, erasing both the Mist and the Ultimate Gate.</li>
<li><b>Now your fleets get their turn.</b> A constrained avatar gives you a 720-day combat window and scales with game difficulty and the Crisis Strength setting. To win, you must defeat <b>the constrained avatar belonging to your endgame</b> before its stay ends. Completing research, defeating an unconstrained avatar, or destroying an avatar summoned by Strike does not count. Run out of time, and the system is still erased.</li>
</ul>

<h3>After Victory</h3>
After defeating Aforgomon, choose <b>Step Beyond the Gate</b> to proceed directly to the ascension ending, or <b>Pause and Contemplate</b> to keep playing as your current empire.
<br>
Deferring grants the free <b>Ascension</b> special project with no time limit. Whenever you're ready, research it for 30 days to revisit the choice. You can defer again if you wish.
<br>
<b>Your existing colonies are removed and your empire transformed only after you confirm ascension.</b> Victory already unlocks Invert Ethics and Erase, even if you defer ascension; both still cost Hyperlogic, and Erase still has a cooldown. Confirming ascension upgrades Strike to its full avatar and makes every DAIT operation free and exempt from cooldowns. Operations with cast times still require you to wait. Empire-wide Strike records witnessing empires with fleets in the central system when casting begins, then spreads through their territory when it resolves. Nomadic witnesses also contribute eligible systems containing their fleets at that time. Each system is struck only once. Avatars remain hostile to all, so don't forget that just because you've ascended.

<h2>Compatibility</h2>

This mod removes celestial bodies and ships. It may remove special planets, systems or ships belonging to other mods and leave their event chains stuck.
<b>CRITICAL WARNING:</b> Do not use modded map types! They may prevent the <i><b>Nameless Mist</b></i> from spawning and block Crisis progression!
<br>

Finally, enjoy the game!
