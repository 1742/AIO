<h2>Overview</h2>
This mod adds a new Become the Crisis path: <i><b>All-in-One</b></i>. Regular empires, Hive Minds and Machine Intelligences can all take this path; it is no longer restricted to Gestalts. Build the Nameless Mist and rewrite reality through Directional Apply Infinity Thesis operations. Completing <i><b>Yog-Sothoth</b></i> begins Aforgomon's pursuit; winning the final battle completes your ascension.
<br>

<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
<li>A new Ascension Perk, <b>All-in-One</b>, and its Become the Crisis path</li>
<li>A new series of <b>Directional Apply Infinity Thesis</b> effects</li>
<li>A new All-in-One Origin</li>
<li>New Crisis megastructures, the <b>Nameless Mist</b>, and a Matryoshka Brain independent of the capital</li>
<li>An <b>Aforgomon endgame</b>, with full DAIT access after victory</li>
</ul>

<h2>Core Gameplay</h2>
Requires The Machine Age DLC, three previously adopted Ascension Perks, and the independence, civic and other requirements shown in the perk panel. Only one empire per game can take this path. Spend <b>Advanced Logic</b> to edit the universe and advance your Crisis progress. As a variant of the Cosmogenesis Crisis, it includes Fallen Empire buildings and ships too, though at their current power and cost efficiency, is anyone really going to use them...? (What patch is this, and we're still spamming repeatables and ships?)

<h2>Walkthrough</h2>
<h3>Getting Started and Megastructures</h3>
After adopting the perk, accumulate <b>Advanced Logic</b>, research the exclusive technologies and build the <b>Horizon Engine</b>. Crisis levels require both accumulated Logic and technological or megastructure milestones; check the Crisis panel for the exact requirements.
<br>
Upgrading the Horizon Engine into the gate on the reality side creates <b>the Nameless Mist</b>, a passage between the two systems, but no colony inside the Mist. Inside the Mist, progress through the <b>Silver Key → Eternal Throne → Yog-Sothoth</b>, unlocking more DAIT operations along the way. Stockpile Energy, Alloys, Dark Matter, Living Metal and Nanites: the later upgrades are not cheap.

<h3>Matryoshka Brain and Apply Infinity</h3>
One Matryoshka Brain district per eligible colony unlocks uncapped Recursive Node jobs. Moving the capital leaves existing Brains intact. Nodes still require population, housing and Energy, and suffer attrition. Virtual pops cannot work these jobs; virtual empires use physical Recursive Chassis instead.
<br>
Enrich, Generate and Storm cost 50000 Hyperlogic; other operations cost 100000. Ascended empires cast for free. At 0/1/2 jumps, costs are multiplied by 1/2/3. All operations have a windup; Seed, Harvest, Strike and Erase also have separate 360-day cooldowns after success. Enrich and Generate change systems directly without country buffs.
<br>
Use the Apply Infinity: Open Menu edict to open the movable toolbar. Select a system or celestial body, then choose an operation and range in the toolbar. The entry does not replace vanilla interface files. The special origin starts directly ascended.

<h3>Endgame: Aforgomon</h3>
<b>Completing Yog-Sothoth immediately begins the endgame; it does not grant victory.</b> Have your science ships, resources and fleets ready before finishing construction.
<ul>
<li>Aforgomon manifests repeatedly, with a shorter warning period each round. After remaining in an external system for 180 days, it erases that system and leaves remnants to investigate.</li>
<li>Investigate <b>1 / 2 / 3</b> different remnants to understand its cycle, unlock Induction, and unlock Constraint respectively. Each investigation takes 60 days and expires after 1000 days. Failed or cancelled projects are not restored.</li>
<li>Induction redirects this round's manifestation to a chosen external system. Constraint also redirects it and makes the manifestation beatable. Both field projects take 60 days and must finish before the warning period ends. Redirecting the target does not reset the countdown.</li>
<li>Defeat the <b>constrained manifestation</b> within its 360-day stay to win. Completing the research alone is not victory. If it reaches the Nameless Mist, the endgame fails and the Mist and the Ultimate Gate are removed.</li>
</ul>
The ascension ending removes your colonies and transforms your empire. Continued play unlocks Ethics Inversion and Erase, and upgrades Strike to its full manifestation. Its realm-wide range records empires with fleets at the target when casting and spreads across their territory when resolving. Strike remains indiscriminate.

<h2>Compatibility</h2>

This mod removes celestial bodies and ships. It may accidentally remove special planets, systems, or ships belonging to other mods and cause their event chains to become stuck.
<b>CRITICAL WARNING:</b> Do not use modded map types! They may prevent <i><b>the Nameless Mist</b></i> from spawning and block Crisis progression!
<br>

Finally, enjoy the game!
